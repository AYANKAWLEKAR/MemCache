# Memory impact evaluation, 2026-09-16

The memcache context is capped at its production default of 1,200 tokens while full_transcript is deliberately uncapped: paying whatever the history costs is that approach's nature, and the token column records the price. Cells are mean (min..max) over repetitions. Coverage is the fraction of required facts present in the answer; retrieval recall is those facts' presence in the retrieved context itself, memcache only.
