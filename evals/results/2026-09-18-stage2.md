# Memory impact evaluation, stage 2 (naive RAG added), 2026-09-18

Conditions share identical seeded sessions within each repetition; only the context mechanism differs. The memcache and naive_rag contexts are both capped at 1,200 tokens (the production default), so the two retrieval approaches compete at an identical budget, while full_transcript is deliberately uncapped: paying whatever the history costs is that approach's nature, and the token column records the price. Cells are mean (min..max) over repetitions. Coverage is the fraction of required facts present in the answer; retrieval recall is those facts' presence in the retrieved context itself, reported for both capped retrieval conditions.
